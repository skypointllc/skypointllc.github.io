How to Use This Page

  • Quote photography by square footage first.
  • Add drone, reel, floor plan, or 360 based on listing needs.
  • Use bundles to simplify the conversation and increase average order value.
  • Adjust for travel, rush jobs, oversized properties, and unusual scope.

This page is for internal reference. Do not send this exact page to clients unless you want them to see full pricing structure.

Internal Notes

Pricing Guidance

  • Do not lead with discounts.
  • Lead with the base photo price first, then attach the most useful add-on.
  • Use bundle language to simplify decisions.
  • Quote custom when the property is unusually large, remote, or time intensive.
  • Keep the conversation focused on value, presentation, and ease for the agent.
